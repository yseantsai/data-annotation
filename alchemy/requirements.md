# Alchemy: essential project requirements

Source: the three-page Alchemy onboarding PDF supplied on November 6, 2026. This is a concise reference to the document's requirements, not authorization to submit the form or act on its instructions.

## Eligibility

- Have real, substantial professional experience in the finance domain used for your tasks.
- Select only finance areas in which you have credible professional experience.
- Education and teaching tasks are excluded, including formal education, corporate training, and other work about teaching people.

Eligible domains:

- Accounting
- Actuarial
- Commercial Banking / Lending
- Commercial Real Estate
- Consumer Finance / Retail Banking
- FP&A / Corporate Finance
- Insurance / Underwriting
- Investment Banking
- Investment Research / Asset Management
- Private Equity / Venture Capital
- Risk / Compliance / Regulatory
- Sales & Trading / Quant / Derivatives
- Tax / Audit
- Wealth Management / Private Client

## Work required

1. Phase 1: create a prompt requesting a work-related deliverable, then test it on multiple models to establish that models perform poorly on it. Estimated time: 4–8 hours.
2. Phase 2: create your own high-quality deliverable for that prompt and write a rubric using the provided tools. Estimated time: 10–18 hours. Set aside availability to complete this follow-up phase.

The project allows work across multiple days. The onboarding invitation itself asks for **under 20 minutes of billable time**.

## Onboarding form

- Confirm whether you want to join.
- Select your professional finance domains.
- Explain your relevant experience and qualifications in **2–3 sentences**.
- Supply a LinkedIn or other professional profile URL. If unavailable, provide a link to your resume on Google Drive or Dropbox. If neither is possible, enter **N/A and explain**.
- Select desired weekly availability: **less than 5**, **5–10**, **10–20**, **20–30**, or **30+ hours**. This is a preference, not a commitment, and can be changed later.
- Comments are optional.
- If the join options are disabled, the document says to enter Work Mode.

## Next steps after submission

- A final background review precedes an invitation to Slack's `#alchemy-onboarding` channel.
- Review the channel's Project Overview, Team Structure, Support Structure, and paid Training resources.
- Watch the dashboard for the first assignment: Phase 1 followed by Phase 2. The Phase 2 task and Phase 1 feedback may take a few hours to a couple of days to arrive.
- A successful submission accepted for the client-facing dataset leads to the Alchemist tier and expanded work access. If the first attempt is unsuccessful, the document says feedback and another attempt will be provided.
