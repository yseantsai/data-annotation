# Finance & Investing: Write a Challenging, Realistic Prompt

## Task

Use an LLM to author and refine a realistic finance or investing prompt, then evaluate the test model's response and complete `submission-form.md`. Human input is optional; AI tools may be used throughout.

- Apply expert-level finance and investing knowledge.
- Phrase the prompt as a natural request grounded in a realistic scenario.
- Use complexity and constraints that serve the scenario. Avoid arbitrary formatting puzzles, random trivia, and minor variations of previous prompts.
- Target a substantive error: an incorrect or misleading answer, or an essential flaw in reasoning or explanation.
- If the response is generally accurate, even with minor errors, revise the prompt or try a different idea and test again.
- Base the rating and explanation on the actual test response.

## Example use cases

- Pricing and forecasting supply and demand for a product or service
- Valuing a company from scratch or for an acquisition
- Evaluating an investment using company and competitor business fundamentals
- Evaluating an investment using market and trading comparables
- Monitoring and managing an investment portfolio toward a goal
- Maintaining bookkeeping compliance with regulations and audits
- Creating business budgets, forecasting revenue and expenses, and analyzing variances against forecasts

## Content requirements

- Avoid adversarial prompts and requests involving safety violations, sensitive topics, crimes, or controversial topics.
- Use fictional personal details rather than private information about real people.

## Testing and submission

1. Enter the prompt in the project interface and click **Generate**.
2. Review the response. Proceed only if it has major issues or an essential flaw that makes it **OK** at best.
3. If the response is **Amazing** or **Pretty Good**, revise the prompt or try another idea and retest.
4. Complete the rating and write at least a few sentences explaining the mistakes and their severity.
5. If errors persist after more than three retries, or no response arrives for 15+ minutes, report the issue in the project chat.
