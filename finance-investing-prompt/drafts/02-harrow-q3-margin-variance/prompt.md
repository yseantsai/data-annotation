Revenue beat budget, but gross margin came in at 21.9 percent against a budget of 27.2 percent. Dale Okafor, our VP of sales, wants the commentary to lead with the steel surcharge from Brennick Steel. His email says: "Brennick's surcharge is $95 a ton since August 1, and with 82 pounds of steel in a standard unit and 176 in a heavy-duty, that's about $135,000 on what we shipped in Q3." - Heavy-duty: budget 6,880 units at $468.90 and $322.15; actual 5,135 units at $472.30 and $331.20.

The plant added Saturday shifts in July and August, and the plant controller puts the overtime premium at $71,380 and expedited inbound freight at $28,940 for the quarter. On steel, we had 690 tons on hand on August 1 after purchasing bought ahead in July. We bought 412 tons at the surcharge price in August and September and ended September with 144 tons. We cost inventory on FIFO.

How much of the margin miss does the surcharge actually explain, what explains the rest, and what should the commentary say? I need numbers I can defend line by line if the CFO asks.
