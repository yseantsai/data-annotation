## Prompt

Below is a transcript of an earnings call for NVIDIA. Provide a summary with a data table. The data table should summarize revenue and percent change numbers YoY and compared to last quarter by division/business unit. Include all major divisions, but smaller ones (<1% total revenue) can be excluded. Give the transcript provided a score out of 100 for its sentiment – 100 for perfectly positive sentiment - 50 for neutral - 0 for perfectly negative sentiment. Explain this sentiment score in one sentence after the text summary.

### NVIDIA Q3 2024 Earnings Call Transcript

Thanks, Simona. Q3 was another record quarter. Revenue of $18.1 million was up 34% sequentially and up more than 200% year on year, well above our outlook of $16 billion. Moving to data center, the NVIDIA HGX platform, based on our Hopper Tensor Core GPU architecture, along with InfiniBand end-to-end networking, drove record revenue of $14.5 billion, up 41% sequentially and up 279% year on year. Demand was strong across consumer internet companies and enterprises, with notable deployments in generative AI applications and a growing enterprise wave of AI adoption.

Moving to gaming. Gaming revenue of $2.86 billion was up 15% sequentially and up more than 80% year on year, driven by strong demand during the back-to-school season. The gaming ecosystem has been significantly enhanced by our RTX and AI technologies, now available at more accessible price points.

Moving to ProViz. Revenue reached $416 million, up 10% sequentially and 108% year on year, driven by the adoption of NVIDIA RTX in professional design and AI workloads. Omniverse continues to be a key platform for digital twins and simulation, enhancing efficiency in sectors like manufacturing. Finally, for automotive, revenue was $261 million, up 3% sequentially and up 4% year on year, primarily fueled by growth in self-driving platforms and AI cockpit solutions. Our partnerships, including those with Foxconn, are expanding NVIDIA's presence in the automotive industry.

We face challenges such as new export control regulations affecting sales in China and other markets, for which we are actively pursuing compliant solutions. Our sales to China and other affected destinations derived from products that are now subject to licensing requirements have consistently contributed approximately 20% to 25% of data center revenue over the past few quarters. Despite this, we are optimistic about compensating this with strong growth in other regions. We expect continued investment in AI and computing infrastructure globally to drive future growth. Looking ahead, total revenue for the fourth quarter is expected to be $20 billion, with strong demand in data centers anticipated to continue.
