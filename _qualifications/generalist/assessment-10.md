# Assessment 10

## Prompt

> Write me a review about an Italian restaurant I went to yesterday called “Pasta World”. The food was okay, but way too salty. The waiter was very rude, but the music was lovely. Everything was very expensive, but the presentation was beautiful and the dessert was delicious. I think the entire experience was a 4/10, and I wouldn’t recommend it. Make it 70 words or less.

## Response A

Pasta World boasts beautiful presentation and a delicious dessert, but that’s where the positives end. It was overly salty! Our server was unpleasant, and the prices were steep. The lovely music couldn’t make up for the overall disappointment. I give it a 4/10 and wouldn’t recommend it.

## Response B

I dined at Pasta World yesterday. While the presentation was beautiful and the music was lovely, the food was overly salty, and the apple pie dessert was downright disgusting. To top it off, the waiter was rude, and the prices were unjustifiably high. Overall, a 4/10 experience. Look elsewhere for Italian cuisine.
