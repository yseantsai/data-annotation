# Assessment 4

## Prompt

> Write me a poem about noodles.

## Response A

```text
Noodles

Noodles are long and thin,
They come in many different shapes.
Some are made from wheat,
Some are made from rice.

Some noodles are boiled,
Some noodles are fried.
Some noodles are served hot,
Some noodles are served cold.

Noodles are a popular food,
They are eaten all over the world.
They are a versatile food,
They can be served with many different toppings.

Noodles are a delicious food,
They are a comfort food.
They are a food that brings people together,
They are a food that makes people happy.
```

## Response B

```text
Noodles, oh noodles, so long and so thin
A staple food, loved by all within
In soups or stir-fry, they're always a hit
With endless flavors, they'll never quit

From ramen to udon, they come in a variety
Each with its own twist, that'll make you say "yum" with glee
From Japan to China, they're known to be grand
In every culture, they hold a special place, in hand

The soft and the chewy, the perfect texture for any dish
With sauces and spices, they'll tantalize any wish
Eaten with chopsticks, or a fork and knife
Noodles are versatile, and always bring joy to life

So here's to the noodles, and their endless delight
For every meal, they'll make everything alright.
```
