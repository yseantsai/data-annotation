### II. Legal Framework and Standard of Proof

Under INA § 204(a)(1)(A)(iii) and [USCIS_Policy_Manual_Volume_3_Part_D_VAWA.pdf](file:///Users/ritaguest/.gemini/antigravity/brain/ae15580b-c451-4215-abc2-fa446bbdb4a2/scratch/input_files/USCIS_Policy_Manual_Volume_3_Part_D_VAWA.pdf) (3 USCIS-PM D.2(A), p. 6), a VAWA self-petitioning spouse of a U.S. citizen must establish the following eligibility requirements:
1. Proper filing of Form I-360 (3 USCIS-PM D.4(A), p. 67);
2. A qualifying spousal relationship to an abusive U.S. citizen or lawful permanent resident (LPR) (3 USCIS-PM D.2(B), pp. 7–10);
3. Entry into the qualifying marriage in good faith (3 USCIS-PM D.2(C), pp. 20–21);
4. Eligibility for immigrant classification as an immediate relative or under a family-based preference category (3 USCIS-PM D.2(D), pp. 21–22);
5. Having been subjected to battery or extreme cruelty perpetrated by the U.S. citizen or LPR spouse during the qualifying marriage (3 USCIS-PM D.2(E), pp. 22–26);
6. Past or present joint residence with the abusive U.S. citizen or LPR spouse (3 USCIS-PM D.2(F), pp. 26–27); and
7. Good moral character (3 USCIS-PM D.2(G), pp. 27–33).

**Standard of Proof and Evidentiary Standard:**
* **Preponderance of the Evidence:** The burden of proof rests on the self-petitioner to establish eligibility by a preponderance of the evidence, meaning that the claim is "more likely than not" or "probably" true ([USCIS_Policy_Manual_Volume_3_Part_D_VAWA.pdf](file:///Users/ritaguest/.gemini/antigravity/brain/ae15580b-c451-4215-abc2-fa446bbdb4a2/scratch/input_files/USCIS_Policy_Manual_Volume_3_Part_D_VAWA.pdf), 3 USCIS-PM D.2(A), pp. 6–7; 3 USCIS-PM D.5(B)(1), p. 72).
* **Any Credible Evidence Provision:** Pursuant to INA § 204(a)(1)(J), 8 CFR § 204.2(c)(2)(i), and 3 USCIS-PM D.5(B)(2) (pp. 72–75), USCIS must consider "any credible evidence" relevant to the self-petition. While USCIS retains sole discretion to determine what evidence is credible and the weight to afford it, self-petitioners are not required to submit any specific form of documentation, and a self-petition may not be denied for failure to submit a particular piece of evidence (such as law enforcement reports or court orders) (3 USCIS-PM D.5(B)(2), pp. 73, 75).
* **Mandatory Approval Upon Establishing Eligibility:** The decision to approve or deny a VAWA self-petition is not discretionary. Where USCIS determines that the self-petitioner satisfies all statutory and regulatory eligibility requirements, USCIS approves the self-petition (3 USCIS-PM D.5(D)(1)–(2), p. 77).

##### B. Analysis of Extreme Cruelty
* **Policy Manual Guidance:** Under 8 CFR § 204.2(c)(1)(vi) and [USCIS_Policy_Manual_Volume_3_Part_D_VAWA.pdf](file:///Users/ritaguest/.gemini/antigravity/brain/ae15580b-c451-4215-abc2-fa446bbdb4a2/scratch/input_files/USCIS_Policy_Manual_Volume_3_Part_D_VAWA.pdf) (3 USCIS-PM D.2(E)(1), pp. 23–25), extreme cruelty encompasses psychological or sexual abuse or exploitation, acts or threatened acts of violence resulting or threatening to result in physical or mental injury, and other abusive actions that "in and of themselves, may not initially appear violent but that are a part of an overall pattern of violence." While "[h]urtful conduct alone, or adverse interactions of limited severity, are not sufficient to establish 'extreme' cruelty," conduct constitutes extreme cruelty where "[t]he actions ... reflect an intention to gain or maintain power and control over the self-petitioner," endanger the physical or mental health of the spouse, and are "sufficiently extreme to implicate the purposes of the statute" (3 USCIS-PM D.2(E)(1), pp. 23–24).
* **Application to the Record:** Assessing the totality of the circumstances (3 USCIS-PM D.2(E)(1), pp. 24–25; 3 USCIS-PM D.5(B)(2), p. 74), the record establishes that from early 2024 through May 2025, Mr. Lattimore subjected Ms. Whitfield to a sustained, escalating pattern of coercive control and psychological abuse that went well beyond ordinary marital discord:

Here, whenever Ms. Whitfield objected to Mr. Lattimore’s mistreatment, he explicitly weaponized her immigration status, telling her that she depended on him for her green card and that "he could have me sent back to Jamaica whenever he wanted" ([Affidavit_Marcia_Whitfield.pdf](file:///Users/ritaguest/.gemini/antigravity/brain/ae15580b-c451-4215-abc2-fa446bbdb4a2/scratch/input_files/Affidavit_Marcia_Whitfield.pdf), p. 3, ¶ 16).
