courtship, and the couple married on July 15, 2022, in East Orange, New Jersey, after which she lived with

9. CLINICAL OPINION
Based on my assessment over eight sessions, Ms. Whitfield’s presentation is consistent with that of an
individual who has been subjected to sustained emotional and psychological abuse by an intimate partner.
Her symptoms began during the marriage, and specifically after the onset of the mistreatment she describes
in early 2024. She has no prior psychiatric history and no other identified stressor that would account for
them. Her account was detailed, internally consistent, and accompanied by appropriate emotional
responses, and I found no indication of exaggeration or symptom fabrication.
In my professional opinion, the pattern Ms. Whitfield describes, including degradation, economic control,
sexual coercion, threats related to her immigration status, and the spitting incident, constitutes a pattern of
psychological abuse and coercive control. This abuse is the primary cause of her current depressive and
posttraumatic symptoms.

therapy (TF-CBT).
