About Me
 1.   My name is Marcia Evadne Whitfield. I was born on March 14, 1984, in Spanish Town, St.
 Catherine, Jamaica. I am a citizen of Jamaica. I live at 418 Prospect Street, Apartment
3B, East Orange, New Jersey 07017. My A-Number is [A-NUMBER].
2.   I make this affidavit in support of my Form I-360 self-petition as the abused spouse of a
 United States citizen, Vernon Lloyd Lattimore. Everything I state here is based on my
own personal knowledge. I read and understand English, which is my first language.
3.   I came to the United States on August 2, 2018, in J-2 status as the dependent spouse of my
 first husband, who came here as a J-1 research scholar. I worked as a home health aide
with the employment authorization I had as a J-2. My first marriage ended in divorce in
June 2021 in Essex County, New Jersey. We simply grew apart, and the divorce was not
contested. We had no children.
How I Met Vernon and Our Marriage
 4.   I met Vernon on Saturday, October 23, 2021, at a birthday gathering for my longtime
 friend Desmond Reid at Desmond's home in East Orange. Desmond and I have known
each other for about twenty years, since we were young people in Spanish Town. Vernon
was a friend of Desmond's from work, and Desmond introduced us that evening. Vernon
was funny and very attentive. He asked for my number before he left.
5.   Vernon and I began seeing each other in November 2021. We went to dinner, to the
 movies, and on long drives down the Shore. He came with me to church a few times and
met my cousin Simone Barrett when she visited from Brooklyn for Christmas. He told me
he wanted a wife and a real home. In March 2022 he asked me to marry him, and I said
yes because I loved him and wanted to build a life with him.
6.   We married on July 15, 2022, at East Orange City Hall. Desmond and Simone were there,
 and afterward we had a small dinner with about fifteen family members and friends. On
the day of our wedding I moved into Vernon's apartment at 418 Prospect Street,
Apartment 3B, and my name was added to the lease. We opened a joint bank account and
shared all of our household bills.

Money, Lies, and My Immigration Case

16.   Vernon knew how important my immigration case was to me, and he began to use it

for my green card and that he could have me sent back to Jamaica whenever he wanted.
Our adjustment interview was scheduled for April 2025. On the morning of the interview,

voice, and I feel nervous when I hear keys at the door, even though Vernon is gone.
