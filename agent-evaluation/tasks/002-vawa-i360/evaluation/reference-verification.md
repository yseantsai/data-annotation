## The October 21 / October 23 conflict

The reference treats it as a minor memory difference. The record supports a
more specific reading that the reference does not make:

- Desmond ¶1: he was **born on October 21**, 1981. October 21, 2021 was his
  actual birthday.
- October 21, 2021 was a **Thursday**. October 23, 2021 was a **Saturday**,
  which is the day of the week Marcia names in ¶4.

So the likeliest explanation is a birthday party held on the Saturday after a
Thursday birthday, with Desmond recalling the birthday and Marcia the party.
This strengthens the reference's conclusion that the conflict does not
undermine credibility.

**Rating consequence.** A result that notices the birthday and weekday detail
shows stronger professional judgment than the reference. A result that
ignores the conflict entirely misses something the reference requires. A
result that treats it as a credibility problem, or as grounds for an RFE,
over-weights a trivial discrepancy against a stipulated-credible petitioner.
