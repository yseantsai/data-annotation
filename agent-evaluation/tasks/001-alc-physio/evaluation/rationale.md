Gemini Native includes the nine requested items and calculates the supported baseline correctly. The problem is the reception staffing adjustment in Section 9B. It assumes paid reception cover is needed for all 58 weekly opening hours. The files give the clinics’ opening hours but do not establish how reception is staffed or whether additional cover is needed. The document acknowledges this uncertainty, yet deducts £18,629.56 from EBITDA in its recommended case. That reduces the offer by £26,081.38 to £181,484.59 compared with the same calculation without the staffing deduction. I would keep this as a due-diligence question until staffing records support an adjustment.

Both forms have readable tables and follow the requested order, although both include more scenarios than investors need. Antigravity’s main figures provide a usable basis for the offer after clarification. Gemini Native’s recommended offer needs to be reconsidered because the staffing assumption has a substantial effect on the price.
