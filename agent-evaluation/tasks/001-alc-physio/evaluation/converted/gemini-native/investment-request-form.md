- Suspected Duplicate Website / Web Support Expense Removed (All Clinics — To Confirm with Seller): Web support services (Row 31, Admin) and Website, SEO & Content (Row 35, Marketing) show identical figures across all sites: £2,104.50 (Clinic 1), £1,904.51 (Clinic 2), and £975.45 (Clinic 3). As the accountant’s draft figures were unchecked, removing one duplicate row adds +£4,984.46 total EBITDA (+£6,773.40 valuation). If confirmed as two separate contracts, valuation reduces by £6,773.40.

B. Adjustments in Our Favour (Decreasing Total EBITDA by -£18,629.56 | Valuation by -£28,077.01)

- Reception / Admin Wage Uplift to National Living Wage for Full Opening Hours (Clinics 1 & 2 — To Confirm with Seller): All clinics open 8am–8pm Mon–Thu (48 hrs) and 8am–6pm Fri (10 hrs) = 58 hours/week (3,016 hours/year over 52 weeks). Recorded Reception / Admin Wages of £28,080.00 (Clinic 1) and £29,229.00 (Clinic 2) imply £9.31/hr and £9.69/hr over 3,016 hours—below the 2025–26 UK statutory National Living Wage (NLW) of £12.21/hr (indicating either unrecorded/owner-covered reception hours or unstaffed reception for ~13–18 hrs/wk).

- Quantified NLW Staffing Impact: Staffing all 3,016 opening hours/year at the £12.21/hr NLW requires £36,825.36 wages + £5,155.55 (14% NI/pension) = £41,980.91 per clinic. This adds £8,745.36 wages + £1,224.35 NI/pension at Clinic 1 (-£9,969.71 EBITDA) and £7,596.36 wages + £1,063.49 NI/pension at Clinic 2 (-£8,659.85 EBITDA), reducing total EBITDA by -£18,629.56 (-£26,081.38 valuation at 1.4x). (Note: At the April 2026 NLW of £12.71/hr, required cost is £43,700.03/site, reducing EBITDA by -£22,067.80 and valuation by -£30,894.92; if partial reception cover is intentional, no deduction is required.)

- 15% Non-Standalone Discount on Clinic 3 (Clinic 3 — Agreed Policy): Applying 1.4x to Clinic 3’s fully adjusted EBITDA (£9,503.00) yields £13,304.20; deducting 15% (-£1,995.63) gives £11,308.57 (effective 1.19x multiple) to reflect non-standalone gym tenure risk.

- Additional Due Diligence Checks: Confirm Clinic 3’s £0 lines for Business Rates, Utilities, Cleaning & Clinical Waste Disposal, and Reception are all included in the £14,400/yr Kennark’s Gym rent (especially clinical waste disposal). Note that consultation (£70 initial / £50 follow-up) and massage (£60 / £40) fees match our pricing (~£88/hr full-book revenue vs our £86.95/hr), so post-acquisition upside will come from room utilisation rather than price rises.
